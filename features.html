Features

Everything Pulse does.

Pulse checks whether AI assistants recommend your brand, helps you fix the gaps, and proves the results.

01 Scan

Ask 12 AI assistants buyer questions about your category.

02 Score

See how visible your brand is, model by model.

03 Optimise

Generate content that AI assistants are more likely to cite.

04 Publish

Push directly to WordPress, Shopify, or export as Markdown.

05 Monitor

Keep scanning automatically and alert you when things change.

06 Verify

Compare before and after to prove the improvement worked.

Evidence record

Every time Pulse asks an AI assistant about your category, it saves the exact question, the full answer, and any sources the AI cited. When a score changes, you can see exactly what the AI said and why. No summaries, no estimates.

Visibility scoring

Pulse scores your brand across six dimensions: whether you were mentioned, your rank in the answer, how strongly you were recommended, the sentiment of the mention, the quality of sources cited, and how consistent your visibility is across different AI models. Each dimension is weighted so the final score reflects what matters most to buyers.

Content writer

When Pulse finds a gap in your visibility, the content writer generates pages, briefs, and structured data designed to help AI assistants cite you. The output is built from the specific gaps Pulse found for your brand, not from generic templates.

Shopping visibility

When buyers ask Amazon Rufus, ChatGPT, or Perplexity "what's the best [product] for [use case]?", does your product show up? This feature tracks whether your products appear in AI shopping recommendations and how that changes over time.

AI crawler tracking

AI companies send bots to crawl websites and use that content to inform their models. Pulse parses your server logs and shows you which AI bots visit your site, which pages they read, how deep they go, and how often they return.

Task management

When Pulse finds a visibility gap, you can turn it into an assigned task for your team. Each task links back to the evidence that flagged it, and Pulse captures a before/after snapshot so you can prove the fix actually worked.

ROI reporting

Connect Google Analytics or Search Console and Pulse maps AI-driven traffic back to specific platforms and landing pages. See which visibility improvements actually brought visitors and conversions, so you can report real results to leadership.

Buyer question discovery

Find out what people are actually asking AI assistants in your category. Import real search data from Google Search Console or SEMRush, or let Pulse discover questions automatically by expanding on your core topics.

Source verification

When an AI assistant cites a source in its answer, Pulse checks whether that source actually exists and whether it says what the AI claims. Hallucinated or broken sources are flagged automatically so you know which citations are real.

Brand identity check

Checks whether AI assistants can correctly identify your brand or whether they confuse it with similarly named companies. Also reviews the structured data on your website to make sure AI models can parse your brand clearly.

Content gap finder

When AI assistants can't recommend you because the right content doesn't exist on your site, Pulse tells you exactly what to create: the specific pages, case studies, or comparison guides that would fill the gap and give AI something to cite.

Multi-brand management

Agencies and teams managing multiple brands get separate workspaces for each one. Every workspace has its own prompts, competitors, alert rules, and reports. Enterprise tier adds white-label branding on client-facing reports.

Developer API

Pull Pulse data into your own tools and workflows. Query visibility scores, retrieve evidence records, and trigger scans from AI coding assistants, CI pipelines, or custom integrations. Works with any tool that supports the MCP protocol.

Cloud or desktop deployment

Every feature works on cloud by default. If your organisation needs to keep data local or use its own API keys, you can run the full platform as a desktop app instead. Same features, same interface, no data leaves your machine.
