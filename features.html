Everything Pulse does

Pulse measures how AI assistants and shopping agents treat your brand and products, turns what it finds into a plan, and checks whether the plan worked. This page walks through each part of the app.

Measure

Each scan asks the assistants real questions, the way your shoppers or buyers would, and reads every answer.

Visibility

How often answers mention and cite you, by assistant and against competitors, with your share of voice and position. Each rate comes with its likely range and sample size.

Audiences

Describe the shoppers you sell to. Pulse asks questions in each one's voice, including follow-ups, and shows who hears about you and who hears about a competitor. Audiences are simulated and every assumption is labelled.

Products and prices

Import your catalogue from your Shopify store, a Google Merchant Center feed or a CSV. See which products are named, the prices AI quotes against your catalogue, and the stores it sends shoppers to.

Shopping agents

Agents get real buying tasks. Pulse records whether they find your store, shortlist your products and pick them, compared with each competitor, and why they chose someone else.

Readiness

Live checks on whether agents and AI crawlers can reach and read your site: product data, structured markup, feeds and robots rules. Import server logs to see which AI bots visit and what they read.

Prompts and demand

The questions people ask AI in your category, ranked by how much there is to gain. Bring in search data from Google Search Console, Semrush or Ahrefs to weight them by real demand.

Competitors and sources

Who is recommended in your place, alerts when that changes, and the third-party sites the answers cite most, so you know which reviews and articles matter.

Answers

Every answer is kept, with the question, the assistant, how it was collected and the sources it cited. Any number in Pulse opens the answers behind it.

Accuracy checks

Pulse flags answers that quote the wrong price or stock, confuse you with a similarly named company, or cite a source that doesn't say what the answer claims.

Improve

Findings become a plan your team can work through, and each change is checked on the next scan.

  • Actions: suggested fixes plus your own, with owners and status
  • Content fixes: page rewrites drafted from the answers
  • Lift: each action's effect, measured scan to scan

Content fixes

Rewrites, FAQs and comparison pages built from the specific answers where you were missed. Review them, then publish to WordPress, Webflow, Contentful or Sanity, or send them anywhere with a webhook.

Alerts

Email and Slack alerts when your visibility drops, a competitor surges, answers start getting facts wrong or AI crawlers get blocked.

Schedules

Run scans automatically at the cadence you choose, so trends build up without anyone remembering to press a button.

Report

Results you can share with leadership or clients, each figure with the sample behind it.

Scan reports

One page per scan: how it was measured, the scores, the evidence, and a share link.

Summaries and executive view

How a brand is doing across all its scans, with written summaries and the numbers leadership asks for.

Impact

Visits, conversions and revenue that came from AI answers, from the analytics you import from Google Analytics 4 and Search Console.

Benchmarks

How scores are spread across your category, market and assistant, with the sample behind each.

Branded share links

Send a client or a stakeholder a read-only report under your own name, logo and colours.

Methodology

A plain account of how Pulse measures, what each label means and where the limits are.

Teams and agencies

Many brands, one workspace

Track your own brands and your competitors, or run client workspaces with a share link per client.

Roles and security

Team roles, single sign-on and SCIM provisioning, and a security log of sign-ins and changes.

API and MCP server

Pull scores and evidence into your own tools, or let an AI assistant query Pulse directly through its MCP server.
