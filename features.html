Everything Pulse does

Pulse measures how AI assistants and shopping agents treat your brand and products, turns what it finds into changes you can make, and checks them on the next scan.

The views below use a sample brand. Every number in Pulse opens the answers behind it.

Visibility

How often answers name and cite you, by assistant and against your competitors, week by week.

  • Mention rate, citations, share of voice and position
  • Each rate with its likely range and the number of answers behind it
  • Hover a week to read what the assistants actually said

Audiences

Describe the shoppers you sell to. Pulse asks questions in each one's voice, with follow-ups, and shows who hears about you and who hears a competitor.

  • Audiences add questions to the same scan
  • Every attribute labelled as your data, a public signal or an assumption
  • Simulated shoppers are always labelled as simulated

  • Works without a catalogue too, from your brand and website

Accuracy

Pulse flags answers that quote the wrong price or stock, confuse you with a similarly named company, or cite a source that doesn't say what the answer claims.

  • The wrong claim highlighted in the answer itself
  • The source it most likely came from
  • Alerts when answers start getting facts wrong

Answers that get you wrong

From this week's scan

"For kids, Fellside's Trail Junior costs about £9 and comes in three colours. Fellside also makes waterproof hiking boots, so it's a good one-stop shop."

PriceYour price is £11.00. The £9 comes from a retailer's old page.

Mix-upFellside doesn't make boots. The answer confuses you with a footwear brand with a similar name.

Fixes

Findings become a list of changes your team can work through. Pulse drafts each one from the answers where you were missed, and the next scan asks the same questions again.

  • Page rewrites, FAQs and comparison pages, drafted for you
  • Publish to WordPress, Webflow, Contentful or Sanity, or anywhere with a webhook
  • Owners and status for each change, plus your own
  • Each change's effect measured scan to scan on Business
