Pulse vs Profound

Choose Profound for breadth at enterprise scale. Choose Pulse for retail depth, published prices and the evidence behind every number.

Profound has become a broad AI marketing platform: it tracks answers across nine engines, sells prompt volume data from consumer panels, reads AI crawler logs, and runs agents that write and publish content. Since September 2026 it sells to brands as a free 7-day trial, then Enterprise on request.

Pulse is narrower and built for retailers: it measures how assistants and shopping agents treat your products and each kind of shopper, keeps the evidence and likely range behind every number, and checks each fix on the next scan.

We checked Profound's public website, docs and pricing on 7 October 2026, and re-checked how it handles audiences on 10 October 2026. This comparison is meant to help you judge fit; check current details with Profound.

Which fits your team

Profound may suit you better if

  • You want one platform for AI visibility, prompt volume research, crawler analytics and agents that produce content.
  • You need volumes of real AI conversations by topic.
  • You're buying at enterprise level and want a dedicated contract.

Pulse may suit you better if

  • You sell products and need prices, stock and stores checked against your own catalogue.
  • You want each kind of shopper's questions written in their own words, with follow-ups and every assumption labelled.
  • You want published pricing, a Free plan to start, and the likely range behind every number.
