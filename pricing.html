Pricing questions

Which assistants are included?

Paid plans choose from seven: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Grok with their own web search, plus Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. Scans start with ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, and you can add the rest. The Free plan uses ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.

What counts as a scan?

One run of your questions across the assistants you've chosen, for one brand. It counts as one scan however many assistants or audiences you include. Scheduled scans and scans you start yourself both count.

Do shopping agents use up scans?

No. Shopping agent runs have their own allowance: 300 a month on Professional and 1,500 on Business. One run is one buying task given to one agent.

Can I try Pulse first?

Yes. Start on the Free plan with no card, and upgrade from Plans and billing when you need more. You can also check one question for free without an account.

Can I change plan later?

Yes. Move up or down at any time from Plans and billing in the app.
