What you get

Features explained in plain English.

No jargon. Here is what each capability does and why it matters.

Evidence record

Every time Pulse asks an AI assistant about your category, it saves the exact question, the full answer, and any sources the AI cited. When a score changes, you can see exactly what the AI said and why.

Shopping visibility

When someone asks Amazon Rufus, ChatGPT, or Perplexity "what's the best [product] for [use case]?", does your product show up? This module tracks whether your products appear in AI shopping recommendations.

Buyer question discovery

Find out what people are actually asking AI assistants in your category. Import real search data or let Pulse discover questions automatically. Useful for deciding which content to create next.

AI crawler tracking

AI companies send bots to crawl websites and use that content to train or inform their models. This module shows you which AI bots visit your site, which pages they read, and how often they come back.

Task management

When Pulse finds a visibility gap, you can turn it into an assigned task. Each task links back to the evidence that flagged it, and Pulse captures a before/after snapshot so you can prove the fix worked.

ROI reporting

Connect Google Analytics or Search Console and Pulse maps AI-driven traffic back to specific platforms and pages. See which visibility improvements actually brought visitors and conversions.

Client workspaces

Agencies and teams managing multiple brands get separate workspaces for each. Every workspace has its own prompts, competitors, alerts, and reports. Enterprise adds white-label branding on reports.

Cloud or desktop

Every tier runs on cloud by default. If your organisation has data residency requirements or you want to use your own API keys, you can run the same platform locally as a desktop app.
