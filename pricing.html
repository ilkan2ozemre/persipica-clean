Pricing questions

Which assistants are included?

Every plan measures ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Grok with their own web search, plus Google AI Overviews and AI Mode. Plans differ in how many brands, scans and team members you get, and in the tools on top.

What counts as a scan?

One run of your questions across the assistants you've chosen, for one brand. Scheduled scans and scans you start yourself both count.

Can I try Pulse first?

Run the free grader to check one question across several assistants straight away, or book a walkthrough and we'll look at your brand with you.

Can I change plan later?

Yes. Move up or down at any time from Plans and billing in the app.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us your brand, your category and what you want to find out, and we'll suggest the right plan.
