Pulse vs Conductor

Conductor is an enterprise SEO and AEO platform: AI search tracking across nine engines sits alongside rank tracking, keyword research, site monitoring, a writing assistant and web analytics. Pricing is usage-based and on request, and AI search tracking starts on the Growth plan. Pulse is a focused product for AI recommendations, built for retailers: results by product and by kind of shopper, shopping agents, and fixes checked on the next scan. It works alongside the SEO tools you already have.

We checked Conductor's public website, docs and pricing on 7 October 2026. This comparison is meant to help you judge fit; check current details with Conductor.

Which fits your team

Conductor may suit you better if

  • You're standardising SEO and AI visibility in one enterprise system.
  • You need AI visibility tied into wider site performance and web analytics.
  • Platform scale and cross-team rollout matter most.

Pulse may suit you better if

  • You want to solve AI recommendations without replacing your SEO suite.
  • You sell products and need product, price and shopping agent results.
  • You want published pricing, a Free plan to start, and a quick setup.

How they differ

Conductor

SEO, web analytics and AI visibility in one enterprise system, with a larger procurement process and rollout across teams.

A focused layer for AI recommendations that's quick to set up. There's no SEO suite or web analytics built in; you keep the ones you have.
