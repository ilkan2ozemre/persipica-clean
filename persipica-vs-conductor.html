Pulse vs Conductor

Conductor positions its Intelligence product as an enterprise platform that unifies AI visibility, traditional search, website performance and business analytics. Pulse is a focused product for AI recommendations: the evidence behind each answer, the sources that shaped it, and results for your products, audiences and shopping agents.

Conductor's positioning was reviewed against Conductor Intelligence, AI mention tracking and pricing support pages on 14 May 2026. This comparison is meant to help you judge fit; check current details with each vendor.

Which fits your team

Conductor may suit you better if

  • You are standardising search and AI visibility inside one enterprise system.
  • You need AI visibility tied directly to wider website traffic and revenue analytics.
  • Platform scale and cross-team consolidation are your main objectives.

Pulse may suit you better if

  • You need prompt-level proof and source-level interpretation for stakeholders.
  • You want to solve the AI visibility problem without replacing your SEO suite.
  • You sell products and need product, price and shopping agent results.

How they differ

Conductor

SEO, web analytics and AI visibility in one enterprise system, with a larger procurement process and onboarding across functions.

Pulse

A focused layer for AI recommendations that is quick to set up. There is no SEO suite or web analytics built in; you keep the ones you have.
