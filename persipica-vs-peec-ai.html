Pulse vs Peec AI

Peec positions itself as an AI search analytics tool for marketing teams and agencies, built around visibility, position, sentiment, prompt setup, citation intelligence and reporting. Pulse overlaps on visibility, and goes further on the evidence behind each change, the sources that shaped it, and product, audience and shopping agent results.

Peec AI's positioning was reviewed against Peec's homepage, docs, visibility metric docs and pricing page on 14 May 2026. This comparison is meant to help you judge fit; check current details with each vendor.

Which fits your team

Peec AI may suit you better if

  • You want a lighter analytics workflow with clear reporting.
  • You are an agency or marketing team running prompt-based monitoring.
  • Interface simplicity and speed to insight are your main criteria.

Pulse may suit you better if

  • You need to show stakeholders the answers and sources behind a visibility shift.
  • You want citations read by type: owned, earned, community and review.
  • You sell products and need product, price and shopping agent results.

How they differ

Peec AI

Gives you the trend: visibility, position and sentiment across prompts and models, in a simple interface.

Pulse

Gives you what drove the change: the answers, the sources behind them, the competitor recommended instead, and what to fix.
