Pulse vs Peec AI

Choose Peec for simple analytics on the engines you pick. Choose Pulse for seven assistants on every paid plan, shoppers, shopping agents and drafted fixes.

Peec is AI search analytics for marketing teams and agencies. Since May 2026 it has added SKU-level ChatGPT shopping analytics, AI crawler logs, GA4 referrals and actions with measured impact. Standard plans track 3 engines of your choice, with more on Enterprise.

Pulse measures 7 assistants on every paid plan, adds results by kind of shopper and shopping agents doing buying tasks, and drafts and publishes the fixes.

We checked Peec AI's public website, docs and pricing on 7 October 2026, and re-checked how it handles audiences on 10 October 2026. This comparison is meant to help you judge fit; check current details with Peec AI.

Which fits your team

Peec AI may suit you better if

  • You want a simple analytics workflow with clear reporting across prompts.
  • You're an agency running prompt-based monitoring for many clients.
  • Three engines are enough for your market.

Pulse may suit you better if

  • You want ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok and both Google AI surfaces on every paid plan.
  • You need to see which kinds of shopper hear about you, and how shopping agents choose.
  • You want fixes drafted and published to your CMS, not only recommended.
