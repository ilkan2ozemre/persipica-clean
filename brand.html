Naming

Persipica is the company. Pulse is the product.

  • PersipicaOne word, with a capital P. Never PersiPica, PERSIPICA in running text, or Persipica AI.
  • PulseCapital P. Write Persipica Pulse the first time you mention it, then Pulse.
  • FeaturesFeature names are ordinary words, not names: "audiences", "shopping agents", "fixes".

About Persipica

Use these when you need to describe us, and adapt them as you need.

One line

Persipica makes Pulse, which shows brands how AI assistants and shopping agents recommend their products, and what to change.

Paragraph

Persipica Pulse measures how AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI answer the questions shoppers ask, and whether they name a brand and its products. It asks each question several times, in the voice of different kinds of shopper, keeps every answer and source behind each result, and shows how certain each figure is. Pulse also checks whether AI shopping agents find, shortlist and choose a brand's products, and drafts changes that it measures on the next scan. It is built for online retailers, consumer brands and the agencies that work with them, and has a free plan.

Wordmark

Our main logo, and the one to use whenever there's room. Use it in ink on light backgrounds and white on dark ones, at its original proportions.

Symbol and app icon

The column, for small spaces such as logo grids. The app icon puts it on slate, for profile pictures and app tiles. Where there's room, use the wordmark instead.

The eye

Colours

Mostly white, stone and ink, with burnt orange as the one accent. Use orange sparingly, to mark what matters. Click a colour to copy its code.

Typography
