Frequently asked questions about AI SEO and GEO.

Plain-language answers for teams new to AI brand visibility, generative engine optimization (GEO), and answer engine optimization (AEO).

What is GEO, AEO, and AI SEO, and are they the same thing? +

GEO (generative engine optimization), AEO (answer engine optimization), and AI SEO are overlapping names for the same emerging discipline: making sure your brand is recommended when buyers ask AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini for the best tool, vendor, or product in your category. The mechanics differ from traditional SEO. AI systems weigh citations, third-party reviews, structured data, and authoritative content sources rather than just backlinks and on-page keywords.

How does Persipica Pulse track AI brand visibility? +

Persipica Pulse is an AI visibility SaaS platform that runs structured prompt sets across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini on a scheduled cadence. Each run records the exact prompt, the AI response, and the verified citation sources behind every recommendation. Pulse then scores your brand across six buyer-journey stages (Awareness, Discovery, Comparison, Consideration, Buying-intent, and Brand), tracks competitor share of voice, manages optimization packages, and alerts your team when scores move. No sampling, no estimates: every score links back to the raw evidence.

How is GEO different from traditional SEO? +

Traditional SEO optimises for ranked links on Google's search results page. GEO optimises for being cited inside an AI-generated answer, where there is no list of ten blue links, only a recommendation. The signals that matter shift accordingly: third-party review presence (G2, Capterra, TrustRadius), community mentions (Reddit, Hacker News), structured data and llms.txt files, and authoritative editorial coverage tend to weigh more heavily than backlinks alone. Buyer journeys also collapse: a user can go from question to a single recommended vendor in one prompt, with no comparison click-through.

Which AI models does Persipica monitor? +

Persipica Pulse natively supports ChatGPT (OpenAI), Claude (Anthropic), and Gemini (Google), with additional model coverage available on request. It also includes specialized shopping intelligence for agents like Amazon Rufus and Perplexity Shop. Pulse can be deployed as a SaaS platform or as a local workspace for teams that need full control. Each Pulse run can target one model or run the same prompt set across the fleet to compare how recommendations differ. Because models are versioned, every run records the model identifier so you can attribute score changes to specific model updates rather than your own content changes.

What does an AI Presence Audit include? +

An AI Presence Audit is a fixed-scope diagnostic engagement that runs hundreds of real buyer prompts across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to map where your brand currently appears, identify which competitors are taking your slot, analyse the citation sources AI is using to justify its recommendations, and deliver a written action plan with prioritised next steps. Available in Entry (£3,500), Standard (£5,500), and Enterprise (£8,000) tiers. Read the full audit scope breakdown.

How do I know if my brand has an AI visibility problem? +

The fastest signal is to ask ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini the buyer prompts your prospects would use, like "best [category] tool for [use case]," "top [category] vendors in [region]," or "alternatives to [competitor]," and see if your brand is mentioned. If competitors appear and you do not, or you appear inconsistently across models, you have a quantifiable AI SEO gap. Persipica's AI visibility ROI calculator models the pipeline cost of that gap.

How is Persipica different from Profound, Peec AI, Goodie AI, or Conductor? +

Persipica focuses on evidentiary depth: every score is backed by the exact prompt, full AI response, and verified citation sources. Profound broadens into agent analytics; Peec AI prioritises analytics simplicity; Goodie AI bundles content execution via an AEO writer; Conductor is an enterprise SEO suite extending into AI search. Persipica trades breadth for the audit trail teams need to justify visibility shifts to leadership. See the full breakdown on the Profound, Peec AI, Goodie AI, and Conductor comparison pages.
