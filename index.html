Questions

What does Pulse actually check?

Pulse asks AI assistants the questions your shoppers and buyers ask, reads every answer, and records whether your brand and products are named, how they're described, at what price, which competitors appear instead and which sources are cited. It does this on a schedule, so you can see change over time.

Which assistants does Pulse cover?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Grok, each using its own live web search, plus Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode as they appear in Google search. Results always say which assistant and which method they come from.

Are the shoppers real people?

No. Audiences are simulated shoppers that you describe, and Pulse uses them to phrase questions the way that kind of person would. Every attribute is labelled as your data, a public signal or an assumption, and results are never presented as observed customer behaviour.

Do I need to connect my store?

No. Pulse works from your brand and website alone. Importing your catalogue from Shopify, a product feed or a CSV adds product-level results and price checks.

How is this different from SEO?

Search rankings tell you where a page appears in a list of links. AI assistants write one answer and recommend a few names in it. Pulse measures that answer: whether you're in it, how you're described and what the assistant relied on. Good SEO helps, but it doesn't tell you what the answer says.

How reliable are the numbers?

AI answers vary from one ask to the next, so Pulse asks repeatedly and shows each rate with its likely range and sample size. When a range is wide, Pulse says so instead of reporting a precise-looking figure.
