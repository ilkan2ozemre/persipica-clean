Changelog

A public record of changes to Pulse and to this website.

October 2026

10 October

  • New look for the whole site: serif headings, a burnt orange accent, and a hand-drawn eye that watches the answers
  • The home page now lets you pick a shopper and an assistant, and explore six views from one sample scan
  • The features page shows each part of Pulse working on a sample brand, in place of app screenshots
  • Plan cards now show each plan's limits, and pricing explains what one scan includes
  • Clarified which assistants Pulse covers: choose from seven, with ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini by default
  • Comparison pages: re-checked how each tool reports results by audience, labelled which Pulse plan includes each feature, and listed the pages we checked

  • Redesigned the whole website to match the Pulse app: same typeface, colours, buttons and spacing

  • Aligned plan limits on the pricing page with the app: scans a month and team members
  • Removed the last references to a desktop version
  • Added the Free plan to the pricing page
  • Re-checked the four comparison pages against each vendor's site on 7 October
  • Updated the privacy policy to cover the free grader
