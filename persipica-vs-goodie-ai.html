Pulse vs Goodie AI

Goodie describes itself as an end-to-end AEO platform that helps teams monitor visibility, optimise presence and measure growth across LLMs and AI search, with content, optimisation actions, attribution and commerce workflows in the same system. Pulse covers the same loop, measure, fix and check, and puts the emphasis on the evidence behind each result and on product, audience and shopping agent measurement.

Goodie AI's positioning was reviewed against Goodie's homepage, visibility monitoring, analytics and attribution, and pricing pages on 14 May 2026. This comparison is meant to help you judge fit; check current details with each vendor.

Which fits your team

Goodie AI may suit you better if

  • You want monitoring to lead straight into AI-optimised content production inside one tool.
  • You want an end-to-end AEO workflow managed by the same platform.
  • Buying diagnosis and execution together is a priority.

Pulse may suit you better if

  • You need to show leadership the exact proof behind a recommendation shift.
  • You want citations split into owned, earned, community and review.
  • You want fixes drafted from the evidence, published to your own CMS, and checked on the next scan.

How they differ

Goodie AI

Monitoring leads into its AEO writer and closed-loop optimisation, so diagnosis and execution are one purchase.

Pulse

Every fix starts from the answers where you were missed, publishes to WordPress, Webflow, Contentful, Sanity or a webhook, and is checked on the next scan.
